How to play
  • Click a highlighted square to move. Pawn rules: forward 1 (2 from home), capture diagonally.
  • Red squares are attacked by enemy pieces. Enemies move after you, using real chess moves.
  • Pepper lets your next move be a knight jump. Curry lets it be a king step. Both can capture.
  • Getting captured costs a heart and knocks you back to your home rank.
  • Reach the gold rank to promote and clear the level. Clear all five to win.
