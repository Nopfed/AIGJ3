How to play
  • Move with arrows, WASD, numpad or hjklyubn — eight directions, one tile a turn. Click a lit tile to walk there a step at a time.
  • Bump a guest to whack them with your utensil. Hold a pantry ingredient first to feed them instead — a fed guest leaves happy.
  • Red tiles are where a guest could reach you next turn. Guests hurt you with forks, insults, and demands for food.
  • Ingredients go in the pantry. Tea, utensils and aprons are used on the spot.
  • Take the stairs down. On the last floor, bump the King to serve the meal — everything in your pantry goes on the plate.
